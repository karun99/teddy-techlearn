MTCSE1101

Mathematical Foundations of Computer Science

Probability, Sampling, Hypothesis Testing, Number Theory & Graph Theory

5 Units Core 3 Credits

Course Objectives

  • Understand the mathematical fundamentals that are prerequisites for courses like data mining, networks, security and machine learning.
  • Develop the mathematical and logical basis for modern computer science techniques.
  • Study various sampling and classification problems.

Course Outcomes

After completing this course, a student will be able to:

  • Apply the rules and theorems of probability theory (e.g., Bayes' theorem) to solve engineering problems and find expectation and variance of random variables.
  • Perform and analyse sampling, means, proportions, variances and estimate population parameters by maximum likelihood.
  • Formulate and test hypotheses about sample means, variances and proportions.
  • Design various ciphers using number theory.
  • Apply graph theory to real-time problems like network routing.
