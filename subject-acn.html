MTCSE1106

Advanced Computer Networks

Network Layer, IP, Transport Protocols, Wireless LANs & Emerging Trends

5 Units Elective 3 Credits

Course Objectives

  • Provide a basic understanding of computer networks starting with the OSI reference model, protocols at different layers with emphasis on IP, TCP and UDP and routing algorithms.
  • Cover CSMA/CD, TCP/IP implementation, LANs/WANs, internetworking technologies, routing and addressing.
  • Provide the mathematical background of routing protocols.
  • Develop familiarity with current research problems and methods in advanced computer networks.

Course Outcomes

After completing this course, a student will be able to:

  • Illustrate reference models with layers, protocols and interfaces.
  • Describe routing algorithms, subnetting and addressing of IPv4 and IPv6.
  • Describe and analyse basic protocols of computer networks and how they assist network design.
  • Describe Wireless LANs, WiMAX, IEEE 802.11, cellular telephony and satellite networks.
  • Describe emerging trends in networks - MANETs and WSN.
