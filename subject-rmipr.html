MTCSE1109

Research Methodology and IPR

Research Process, Literature, Ethics, Intellectual Property & Patents

5 Units Core 2 Credits

Course Objectives

  • Understand the process of framing research problems and conducting investigation.
  • Learn effective literature studies, technical writing and research ethics.
  • Understand intellectual property, patents and related rights.

Course Outcomes

After completing this course, a student will be able to:

  • Formulate research problems and design the scope and objectives of a study.
  • Undertake effective literature review, write reports and develop research proposals.
  • Apply knowledge of patents, designs, trademarks and copyright.
  • Understand the process of patenting and international cooperation on IP.
  • Analyse new developments in IPR including IPR of software and biological systems.
