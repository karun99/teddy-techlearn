MTCSE1205

Soft Computing

Fuzzy Sets, Optimization, AI, Neuro-Fuzzy Modeling & Applications

5 Units Elective 3 Credits

Course Objectives

  • Introduce soft computing concepts and techniques to design appropriate techniques for a given scenario.
  • Implement soft-computing-based solutions for real-world problems.
  • Give knowledge of non-traditional technologies: artificial neural networks, fuzzy sets, fuzzy logic, genetic algorithms.
  • Provide hands-on experience with MATLAB to implement various strategies.

Course Outcomes

After completing this course, a student will be able to:

  • Elaborate fuzzy logic and reasoning to handle uncertainty in engineering problems.
  • Make use of genetic algorithms for combinatorial optimization problems.
  • Distinguish artificial intelligence techniques including search heuristics, knowledge representation, planning and reasoning.
  • Formulate and apply the principles of self-adapting and self-organizing neuro-fuzzy inference systems.
  • Evaluate and compare solutions by various soft computing approaches for a given problem.
