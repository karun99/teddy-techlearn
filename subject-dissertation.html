MTCSE2201

Dissertation - Phase I & II

Research, implementation, report and viva-voce

5 Units Dissertation 16 Credits

Course Objectives

  • Ensure a direct link between education, national development and productivity.
  • Address topics relevant to society, industry, national importance, and research and development.

Course Outcomes

After completing this course, a student will be able to:

  • Synthesize knowledge and skills to execute an in-depth study of a new technical problem.
  • Select from different methodologies and methods to produce a suitable research design.
  • Present the findings of a technical solution in a written report.
  • Present the work at a national/international conference or in reputed journals.
