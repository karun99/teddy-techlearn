MTCSE1201

Machine Learning

Supervised, Statistical, SVM, Neural Networks & Decision Trees

5 Units Core 3 Credits

Course Objectives

  • Develop an appreciation for what is involved in learning from data.
  • Demonstrate a wide variety of learning algorithms.
  • Demonstrate how to apply a variety of learning algorithms to data.
  • Demonstrate how to perform evaluation of learning algorithms and model selection.

Course Outcomes

After completing this course, a student will be able to:

  • Gain domain knowledge for productive use of machine learning and diversity of data.
  • Demonstrate supervised and computational learning.
  • Analyse statistics in learning techniques and logistic regression.
  • Illustrate support vector machines and the perceptron algorithm.
  • Design multilayer perceptron networks and classification of decision trees.
