Programme Structure

Choose a semester to explore its subjects. Each subject is broken into accessible unit topics (1.1 → 1.5) with a built-in bank of the most repeated JNTU examination questions per unit and overall.

How to Use This App

1. Study the Unit

Open any subject and click into a unit. Each unit is split into 5 focused topics (1.1 to 1.5) so you can learn in short, digestible sessions.

2. Review Repeated Questions

Every unit lists the most repeated JNTU questions from previous years, plus an overall subject set — focus effort where it counts.

3. Test Yourself

Complete the unit activity (quiz). Score at least 40% to mark the unit complete and unlock the next unit.

4. Track Your Progress

Your scores are saved on this device. Watch your overall progress grow and revisit weak units anytime.
