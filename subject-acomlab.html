MTCSE1107

Advanced Computing Lab

Raspberry Pi, Arduino, MapReduce, Spark & algorithms

5 Units Lab 2 Credits

Course Objectives

  • Provide hands-on experience in using sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke and light and controlling web cameras, networks and relays connected to the Pi.
  • Develop skills for high-quality academic and industrial research in sensors and IoT.

Course Outcomes

After completing this course, a student will be able to:

  • Gain hands-on experience using various sensors and controlling devices connected to the Pi.
  • Develop and use IoT technology in societal and industrial applications.
  • Classify real-world IoT design constraints and industrial automation in IoT.
