Ad Hoc & Sensor Networks

MANETs, MAC Protocols, Routing, WSN Architecture, Localization & QoS

5 Units Elective 3 Credits

Course Objectives

  • Architect sensor networks for various application setups.
  • Devise appropriate data dissemination protocols and model link cost.
  • Understand the fundamental concepts of wireless sensor networks and protocols at various layers.
  • Evaluate the performance of sensor networks and identify bottlenecks.

Course Outcomes

After completing this course, a student will be able to:

  • Explain the fundamental concepts and applications of ad hoc and wireless sensor networks.
  • Discuss the MAC protocol issues of ad hoc networks.
  • Enumerate the concept of routing protocols for ad hoc wireless networks with respect to TCP design issues.
  • Analyse and specify the concepts of network architecture and MAC layer protocols for WSN.
  • Discuss WSN routing issues by considering QoS measurements.
