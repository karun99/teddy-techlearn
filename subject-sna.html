MTCSE2102

Social Network Analysis

Network Measures, Random Graphs, Diffusion, Small World & Game Theory

5 Units Elective 3 Credits

Course Objectives

  • Provide essential knowledge of network analysis applicable to real-world data, with examples from today's most popular social networks.

Course Outcomes

After completing this course, a student will be able to:

  • Demonstrate social network analysis and measures.
  • Analyse random graph models and navigate social network data.
  • Apply network topology and visualization tools.
  • Analyse experiments with small-world models and clustering models.
  • Compare application-driven virtual communities with social network structure.
