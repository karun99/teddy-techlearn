MTCSE1105

Advanced Operating Systems

Distributed, Multiprocessor & Database Operating Systems

5 Units Elective 3 Credits

Course Objectives

  • Provide comprehensive and up-to-date coverage of major developments in distributed, multiprocessor and database operating systems.
  • Cover important theoretical foundations including process synchronization, concurrency, event ordering, mutual exclusion, deadlock, agreement protocols, security, recovery and fault tolerance.

Course Outcomes

After completing this course, a student will be able to:

  • Illustrate the fundamental concepts of distributed operating systems, their architecture and distributed mutual exclusion.
  • Analyse deadlock detection algorithms and agreement protocols.
  • Make use of algorithms for implementing DSM and its scheduling.
  • Apply protection and security in distributed operating systems.
  • Elaborate on concurrency control mechanisms in distributed database systems.
